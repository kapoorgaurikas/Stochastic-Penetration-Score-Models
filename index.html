Mathematical guide

Bernoulli model

E[Xᵢ] = p, Var(Xᵢ) = p(1−p); E[Sₙ] = np, Var(Sₙ) = np(1−p).

Therefore, E[Sₙ/n] = p and Var(Sₙ/n) = p(1−p)/n. The average converges in probability to p. The variance of Sₙ/√n is p(1−p), but its mean is p√n, so the uncentered normalized total does not converge to N(0,1) when p > 0.

Rademacher model

E[Xᵢ] = 0, Var(Xᵢ) = 1; E[Sₙ] = 0, Var(Sₙ) = n.

Thus E[Sₙ/n] = 0 and Var(Sₙ/n) = 1/n, so the average converges in probability to zero. Also, E[Sₙ/√n] = 0 and Var(Sₙ/√n) = 1. By the Central Limit Theorem, Sₙ/√n converges in distribution to N(0,1).

Interpretation caution: This is a simplified stochastic score model. It does not include real-world network topology, attacker coordination, system dependence, defensive controls, or attack rates. Those require additional assumptions.
